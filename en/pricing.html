FAQ

Can we see our opponents' data?

No. TacLog is only for reviewing your own team. There is no way to look up other teams before a match.

Can we change plans later?

Any time. Going back to Free keeps your data (only the visible period gets shorter).

Can we cancel any time?

Yes, from the settings screen. You will not be charged after the next renewal date.

Are recordings uploaded?

No. Recordings are read inside your PC, and only the results (text data) are saved.
